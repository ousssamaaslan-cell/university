# Assets And PDFs

Use authorized university assets and retain their source or license information. Store site imagery and icons under `assets/`; avoid unplanned hotlinks. Keep fonts minimal and accessible. Store published PDFs only under `pdfs/S3/<module-id>/` or `pdfs/S4/<module-id>/` and follow `.claude/rules/pdf-storage.md`. Never replace a PDF silently while leaving stale catalogue metadata.
